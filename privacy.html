Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

kanso uploader is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • Data accessed: the owner's YouTube channel information, and the videos, thumbnails and captions the owner uploads, through the YouTube Data API with the owner's consent.
  • Use: only to upload and update the owner's own videos.
  • Storage: access tokens are kept only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
